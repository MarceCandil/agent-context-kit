# Feature Development Workflow

## Step 1 — Clarify
- Restate goal, constraints, and acceptance criteria.
- Identify files/modules likely impacted.

## Step 2 — Plan (3–8 steps)
- Break down into small increments.
- Include verification checkpoints.

## Step 3 — Implement incrementally
- Make the smallest change that moves the needle.
- Keep diffs localized.

## Step 4 — Verify
- Run: lint/typecheck/tests (as applicable).
- Provide a short manual QA checklist.

## Step 5 — Prepare PR summary
- What changed, why, risks, and how to verify.
