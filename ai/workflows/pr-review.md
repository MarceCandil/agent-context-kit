# PR Review Workflow

## Step 1 — Understand intent
- What is the goal? Are acceptance criteria met?

## Step 2 — Code quality
- Readability, naming, structure, duplication, complexity.

## Step 3 — Correctness & edge cases
- Errors, empty/loading, permissions, fallback paths.

## Step 4 — Tests & verification
- Are tests sufficient?
- Are manual steps documented?

## Step 5 — Risk
- Identify risks and propose mitigations.
